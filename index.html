About Us

A Sanctuary by the Sea

Surf Del Mar Hotel is Sri Lanka's premier coastal retreat — where the rhythm of the ocean sets the pace of life. We blend luxury accommodation with world-class surf experiences in one of Asia's most celebrated surf destinations.

Whether you're chasing waves at dawn or unwinding in your beautifully appointed room after sunset, Surf Del Mar offers an experience that stays with you long after you leave.
